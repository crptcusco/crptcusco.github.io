## 🧠 About Me
I am a Computer Scientist specialized in **Computational Neuroscience**, **Boolean Dynamical Systems**, and **High‑Performance Computing**.  
My work focuses on:

- Attractor computation in **Coupled Boolean Networks (CBNs)**
- Gene Regulatory Networks and biological modeling
- EEG signal processing using discrete neural frameworks
- Parallel and distributed computing in Python (Parsl, Dask, Numba)

I hold a **Ph.D. in Computer Science (UFABC, Brazil, 2024)**, where I developed new algorithms for attractor field computation in large-scale coupled networks.
